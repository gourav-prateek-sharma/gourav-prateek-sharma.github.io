I am an Assistant Professor in the [Electronics and Communications Engineering](https://nitkkr.ac.in/electronics-communication-engg/) department at National Institute of Technology Kurukshetra, India. My research focuses on dependable time-critical communications across wired-wireless systems, with emphasis on time-sensitive networking, deterministic networking, and the integration of these technologies with wireless systems for emerging 5G/6G and cyber-physical applications. I work on problems at the intersection of communication networks, control, and data-driven system design.

## Recent highlights

- Accepted paper at *SPCOM 2026*: "Robust Base Station Placement in Agricultural IoT via Bayesian Optimization"
- Accepted paper at *VTC2026-Fall*: "Modeling and Validation of Quality of Control for Edge-Offloaded Collaborative Navigation"
- Recent journal paper in *IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics*: "Quality of Control-Based Control-Communication Co-Design for Collaborative Robotics"

## Research themes

- Wireless TSN and Deterministic Networking for 5G/6G environments
- Data-driven KPI modeling in mobile networks
- Quality-of-control-aware communication for collaborative robotics and edge systems
- Communication-aware optimization for agricultural IoT and large-scale sensing systems

## Short bio

Before joining NIT Kurukshetra, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the [ISE](https://www.kth.se/is/ise/division-of-information-science-and-engineering-1.790272) division at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Sweden, where I worked with [Prof. James Gross](https://www.kth.se/profile/jamesgr). I received my Ph.D. from [IDLab](https://www.ugent.be/ea/idlab/en), Ghent University, in June 2022 under the supervision of [Prof. Wouter Tavernier](https://woutertavernier.net/) and [Prof. Didier Colle](https://www.ugent.be/ea/idlab/en/members/didier-colle.htm). My Ph.D. [dissertation](https://gourav-prateek-sharma.github.io/files/phd_gsharma_ugent.pdf) focused on optimization algorithms for efficient resource allocation in telecom and media broadcast networks.

I completed my master's in Optoelectronics and Optical Communications from IIT Delhi in 2017 and my bachelor's in Electronics and Communication Engineering from NIT Srinagar in 2015.
